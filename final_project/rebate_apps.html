Rebate Apps

Another aspect of couponing, as I breifly mentioned in another section, is rebate apps. Rebate apps are another great way to save money, some people strictly use rebate apps instead of coupons. There's a lot to learn about rebate apps, but this is just a brief and basic explanation of them, and just a few I've personally used.

Top Rated Coupon Rebate Apps and Websites

  1. Ibotta: earn cash back for purchasing specific items and quanities.
  2. Fetch Rewards: earn points for scanning your receipts, and additional points for specific items to redeem for gift cards.
  3. Checkout51: earn cash back for purchasing specific items, getting gas, taking surveys and shopping online.
  4. Swagbucks: earn points for purchasing specific items, playing games and taking surverys to redeem for gift cards
  5. Stocking Spree Rewards: website specific to Publix, submit receipts after purchasing certain brand items for points towards a Publix gift card.

What to do Know That You Have Downloaded Rebate Apps

After downloading a rebate app, or visiting a rebate website, you need to create an account. Some of these allow you to link your store rewards account to assist in redeeming rebate offers. Most of the apps/websites will require you toupload or take a picture of your receipt after purchasing the item you're looking to get a rebate or points for. Make sure, like the coupons, you pay attention to the fine print. Some apps have offers that require you to purchase a certain flavor, scent, size or quanitiy to receive the rebate. Some apps/websites have different offers or different products for different stores. I suggest doing some research on the apps and websites before starting to submit your receipts so you have a good idea what to expect. Some offers (especially) on Ibotta, specify if you're allowed to stack the rebate offered on the app with a coupon that you used in store. So again, just make sure you're paying extra close attention to the fine print on everything you're doing. These apps do have minimum amounts for cash outs. Some of them offer cash back that gets deposited right into your bank account or will mail you a check. Others only allow you to redeem your points for digital gift cards. Depending on how often you use these apps, and what offers you take advantage of will determine how quickly you acquire enough points to redeem.

A Quick Overview of Two of My Favorite Apps

Fetch is my favorite because I can scan literally ANY receipts and earn a minimum of 25 points per receipts, you can even link your emial, Amazon, and Walmart accounts to redeem digital receipts for points! Over the years I"ve started saving my points up and redeeming them some time around October and use the gift cards for Christmas shopping. Some people I know use their points to use on vacations. Another one of my favorites is Upside, which offers cash back at some gas stations and restaurants. I go to Dunkin Donuts 3 times a week to get coffee for me and my best friend (also my boss), I have a few cards linked to it so I don't even have to scan my receipts. I just open the app, it automatically pulls up offers for whatever businesses I'm closest to, click the offer and claim it, select upload receipt or verify with linked card. In the next 24-48 hours you'll have the cash back you earned in your Upside account.

Other Money Saving/Cash Back Offering Apps and Websites

  • Shopkick: scan items UPC codes in store to earn points
  • Rakuten: provides cashback on online purchases
  • Receipt Hog: earn points for scanning receipts
